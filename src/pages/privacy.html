Privacy notice

Preview notice: registration is currently closed. Signup protection is being configured before the waitlist opens.

ParlonsMoi is developing a French-learning community. This notice describes the information used for its early-community waitlist, not a launched social platform.

What the waitlist asks for

Your full name, email address, country or territory, and self-reported French level. A registration identifier and submission time are created automatically. We do not ask for a password, payment details, date of birth, or precise location.

Why we use it

To manage your request to hear about ParlonsMoi’s development and launch, and to understand the countries and proficiency levels represented in the early community. Submitting the form asks us to contact you for this purpose. It does not create a platform account.

Storage and access

Registrations are stored in Supabase. The site does not provide visitors with a list of registrations or disclose whether a particular person has registered. Access is restricted to authorised administration and the server functionality needed to process the form. Data should not be used for unrelated marketing or sold.

Spam protection and technical data

When signup is enabled, Cloudflare Turnstile checks submissions for automated abuse. Cloudflare processes browser and network information under its privacy policy. The server temporarily keeps a keyed, unreadable representation of an email address to limit repeated submissions. It does not keep a raw IP address in the waitlist tables.

Infrastructure providers may process request metadata in their own operational logs. This website adds no advertising trackers or analytics and does not store signup details in your browser.

Retention and removal

You can ask to be removed from the waitlist using the contact page. The operator will review the waitlist at least every 12 months and delete information that is no longer needed. Backups and provider logs follow their providers’ retention settings.

Questions or corrections

Contact ParlonsMoi to request access, correction, removal, or withdrawal of your request for updates. The operator may need to verify that you control the relevant email address before acting. Contact details are on our contact page.
