01 / The gap

You know the words.
Now you need a conversation.

You can finish a lesson, remember a rule, and still hesitate when someone speaks to you. Knowing French and feeling comfortable using it are different things.

What’s often missing is the chance to take part: to ask a question, tell a story, or simply talk about your day. That’s the space ParlonsMoi is being built for.

02 / The approach

Less watching from the sidelines.
More joining in.

Our starting point is simple: make French part of your life through immersion, participation, and people.

01

Surround yourself
with French.

A French-first environment, with French at the heart of the interface and community. A place to get familiar with the language by spending time in it.

02

Have something
real to say.

Everyday interests, experiences, and questions give you a reason to participate. Progress begins with using the French you already have.

03

Find confidence
through connection.

Learning alongside people who understand the hesitation. Our aim is to bring learners together and, over time, connect them with fluent and native speakers.

03 / The community we imagine

Bring your French.
Bring yourself.

You don’t need a perfect accent or all the right words to have something worth sharing.

We’re shaping a social space where French learners can take part in everyday conversations, share experiences, and build connections around what interests them.

This is our direction for the platform. Community features are still in development.
